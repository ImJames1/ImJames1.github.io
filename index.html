About Me

Hi, I’m James! I’m a SoCal-based designer and front-end developer who loves building thoughtful, seamless digital products. With a background in Computer Science (BS from UC Irvine) and advanced training in visual technology (MS in Graphic Information Technology from ASU), I tackle design challenges with both a creative eye and a developer's mindset. My toolkit spans everything from Figma and Adobe Creative Cloud to responsive HTML5/CSS3, JavaScript, and WordPress. Currently, I’m crafting a web experience as the Lead Web Designer for Grimorium. Off the clock, I’m a big fan of all things pop culture like Marvel movies, Harry Potter and Hunger Games novels, and attending concerts.
